# Preview upcoming events for a portfolio

Report the next dated events relevant to the requested holdings and what the investor can learn from each. Use the requested period and timezone. If the horizon is unspecified, show the next 30 calendar days from a stated as-of date. The result is a dated calendar in the answer.

1. Establish coverage from supplied securities or `list_positions(account=...)` for the selected Trading 212 accounts. Check the environment via `trading_status`. Match issuer, exchange and share class, particularly for ADRs and share-class-specific fund distributions. Keep private positions and amounts local. Search official sites using public issuer or instrument identifiers individually.
2. Confirm each event with the issuer's investor-relations calendar or announcements, an exchange or regulator notice, or the fund manager's distribution or corporate-action notice. An annual report’s year-end date is distinct from its publication date. A previous year's reporting pattern or a third-party calendar can suggest an estimated window. Keep it separate from confirmed dates. Record when the calendar was checked and link the exact evidence. With supplied material only, report the dates it actually establishes. [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) · [FCA National Storage Mechanism](https://www.fca.org.uk/markets/primary-markets/regulatory-disclosures/national-storage-mechanism)
3. Preserve the issuer's local date, exchange timezone and any specified release time. Convert exact times into the user's timezone including the date if conversion crosses midnight. Keep “before open” and “after close” as session labels if the issuer gave no clock time. Distinguish an earnings release, call and vote covering the same announcement. If a new notice supersedes an old one, use the revised date and link the update.
4. For earnings, give one metric or decision worth checking, its previous comparable value or management-guidance range if available, and why it matters to this business. Label any consensus estimate with its source, date, currency, period and accounting basis. If estimates aren't available from a free or supplied source, use published prior guidance. A scheduled event has no reported outcome yet.
5. For dividends or fund distributions, distinguish declaration, ex-dividend, record and payment dates. Use the official dates and share-class terms. If a user requests an estimated payout, multiply the declared per-unit amount by **verified eligible units** in the payment currency. State gross or net basis, tax, ADR ratio and FX assumptions when relevant. A holding observed after the ex-date cannot establish entitlement on its own. [Investor.gov on ex-dividend dates](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/ex-dividend-dates-when-are-you-entitled-stock-and)

Sort confirmed events chronologically. Include the holding, event, local time or session, evidence link and question to revisit. List plausible estimated windows separately if useful. Give the count of securities checked and identify holdings with no confirmed announcement in the sources reviewed. A fund constituent's earnings matter indirectly through its dated fund weight. If highlighting exposure, calculate it once and show any uncovered portion.
